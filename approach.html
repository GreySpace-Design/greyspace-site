HOW WE WORK

From promising to repeatable.

THE WORK BETWEEN

The critical gap is rarely a single broken part. It's the unresolved interfaces between design intent, suppliers, assembly, test, and the maintenance teams who have to keep it running.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Find the actual constraint.

Establish where the product stands, what evidence exists, and what is preventing the next build.

02 / RESOLVE

Close the gaps.

Work across design, electrical integration, sourcing, build methods, and test to turn uncertainty into decisions.

03 / TRANSFER

Make it repeatable.

Leave behind clear build definitions, acceptance criteria, and a path another team can execute.

We usually work closest to the transition from CAD and proof of concept into early production. When the problem extends upstream into design or downstream into manufacturing, we follow the constraint.
