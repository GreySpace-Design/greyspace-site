HOW WE WORK

From promising to repeatable.

The critical gap is rarely a single broken part. It is the unresolved interfaces between design intent, sourcing, assembly, and test.

THE DEVELOPMENT CONTINUUM

From engineering definition to buildable reality.

Good hardware requires more than a complete model. We connect design decisions to suppliers, assembly methods, integration, and validation.

OUR METHOD

01 / UNDERSTAND

Find the actual constraint.

Establish what exists, what evidence supports it, and what prevents the next build.

02 / RESOLVE

Close the gaps.

Work across design, suppliers, assembly methods, integration and test to turn uncertainty into decisions.

03 / TRANSFER

Make it repeatable.

Leave behind build definitions, acceptance criteria, and a path another team can execute.

We are strongest in the transition from engineering design to working, repeatable hardware. We engage upstream or downstream when the constraint requires it.

THE OUTPUT

Engineering another team can execute.

The deliverable isn't just a working prototype. It's the decisions, definitions, methods, and evidence needed to build, test, and transfer hardware with confidence.

BUILD-READY ENGINEERING

Make the definition usable.

Drawings, BOMs, interface definitions, assembly methods, and controlled changes.

SUPPLIER & MANUFACTURING READINESS

Make the build feasible.

RFQ packages, manufacturability resolution, tooling requirements, and supplier handoffs.

INTEGRATION & ACCEPTANCE

Make the result verifiable.

Integration plans, test procedures, acceptance criteria, and documented issue closure.

TRANSFERABLE EXECUTION

Make the work repeatable.

Build documentation, configuration records, open-issue disposition, and production handoff.

Outputs are selected according to the scope and maturity of each project.

WORKING TOGETHER

Start with the constraint.

Whether the problem is already defined or still emerging, we begin by understanding the hardware, the evidence, and the milestone at risk.

01 / ESTABLISH

Establish the problem.

We review the current state, known issues, available evidence, and the next critical build or test milestone.

02 / SCOPE

Agree on the work.

We define a bounded work package with responsibilities, deliverables, and completion criteria. Where uncertainty prevents a credible scope, we can begin with a focused technical assessment.

03 / EXECUTE

Execute and transfer.

We work with your engineers, suppliers, and manufacturing partners to resolve the constraint and integrate the results into your existing systems.

For programs requiring sustained cross-functional support, GreySpace can also embed senior engineering capability within your team.
